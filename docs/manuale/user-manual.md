# SunTrace 2 — User manual

SunTrace 2 tells you what it will cost to heat the room you're about to rent,
in euro a month, before you sign. This manual follows the app step by step,
with a screenshot for each step. The screenshots show the Italian interface;
switch to English with **IT / EN** in the app.

## 1. Who it's for

SunTrace 2 is for people renting in Italy: students, working students,
workers away from home. In a minute you get:

- **the most likely figure** for heating each month, with the range from best to worst case;
- **the share each**, if you split the bills;
- **how much the sun saves you** through the windows;
- **a comparison with a plug-in electric heater**;
- **what to check and ask** at the viewing;
- **a sheet to share** with flatmates.

All you need is a browser, on a computer or a phone. No install, no account.
It only works in Italy. If your device is in dark mode, so is the app.

## 2. Opening SunTrace 2

The home page explains in brief what the app does and already has the search bar.

![The SunTrace 2 home page](01-landing.jpg)

1. Type the address of the home and press **Vai** (Go).
2. If you're already outside the home, press the **crosshair** button: the app uses your location.
3. Top right, switch between **Italian** and **English** (IT / EN).

## 3. The first-visit guide

The first time you open the app, the screen dims and an amber frame circles
one element at a time, with a card explaining what it's for. While the guide
is open you can't use the app.

![The guide: the amber frame circles the search](01b-guida.jpg)

- **Next** and **Back** move through the steps (also with the arrow keys); **Skip** closes it.
- It appears only once. To see it again, use the **Guide** link at the bottom of the column (on a phone, at the bottom of the questions panel).

## 4. Finding the home and drawing the room

Type the address: suggestions appear after three letters. Click the right one
and press **Go**; the map centres on the address. **Enter** only closes the
suggestions.

![Suggestions while typing the address](02-ricerca.jpg)

Zoom in with **+** until you can see the building clearly. On a phone the map
goes closer, so you can tap the corners with a finger.

![The map centred on the address](03-mappa.jpg)

1. Click **once for each corner** of the room, going round it in order.
2. To close it, click **the first point again**. At least three points are needed.
3. In the **Piano** (Floor) bar at the top, pick the floor (T = ground, 5+ = fifth or higher).
4. The **bin** clears the room.

![Three corners placed: the fourth is missing, then the first again](04-disegno.jpg)

**The app refuses drawings that make no sense**, clears them and tells you why:

- sides that **cross**, or a room under **2 m²**;
- a room drawn **on water** (river, lake, sea), with 1.5 m of slack at the shore;
- a room **outside Italy** (the Vatican and San Marino are accepted).

If you draw more than 120 m², the app warns you that you've probably taken in
more than one room.

## 5. Reading the estimate

As soon as you close the room, «Controllo che la stanza non sia sull'acqua…»
(checking the room isn't on water) appears for a few seconds. Then the
**Scaldare questa stanza** (Heating this room) card shows the estimate.

![The estimate right after drawing, with the check message at the bottom](04b-controllo-acqua.jpg)

| Line | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **About … € a month** | The most likely figure: typical values for everything you haven't said yet. |
| **Range** | From best to worst case. While answers are missing it's wide, and the app says so. |
| **When** | The months and hours a day heating runs, by the comune's climate zone. |
| **Each** | Everyone's share, if you enter more people. |
| **The sun** | The most the sun through the windows can save you, shadows included. |
| **With a plug-in heater** | What it would cost with a plug-in electric heater. |
| **Why** | Square metres, outside walls and which way they face, the glazing you entered. |
| **Sources** | This month's ARERA prices for your part of Italy, and the climate source. |

It's an estimate for **comparing homes**, not a quote.

## 6. Answering the viewing questions

Below the estimate there are seven questions: things you can **see or ask at
the viewing**. Each answer sharpens the estimate; if you don't know, leave
**Non so** (Don't know).

![The viewing questions, filled in](06-domande.jpg)

| Question | How to find out |
| --- | --- |
| **How is the home heated?** | Ask the landlord. The boiler is usually in the kitchen, the bathroom or on the balcony. It's the answer that changes the estimate most. |
| **What glazing do the windows have?** | Phone torch on the glass: 2 reflections = single glazing, 4 = double. A purple reflection = low-emissivity. |
| **When was the building built?** | In the listing or the energy certificate (APE). Walls from before 1976 have no insulation. |
| **Above / below the room there is…** | Another flat, or a roof or cellar (they lose more heat). |
| **How many hours a day is it on?** | 4 to 14 hours. The law sets a maximum per zone (14 in Milan, 12 in Rome, 10 in Naples); "Don't know" uses that. |
| **How many people share the bills?** | For the share each. |

After the answers the estimate settles:

![The estimate after the answers: a lower figure, share each, 10 hours a day](07-stima-risposte.jpg)

**Central heating:** the app shows the room's estimated consumption, as with a
gas boiler. What you actually pay also depends on how the building splits the costs.

## 7. The checklist and the shareable sheet

**Alla visita: controlla e chiedi** (At the viewing: check and ask) is a list
of up to five points, different for every room: for example looking for mould
if the room gets little sun, or a reminder that a plug-in heater costs much
more than gas. The last item is always the energy class (APE), which by law
must be in the listing.

**Condividi la scheda** (Share this sheet) copies a link with the room, floor
and answers. Whoever opens it sees the same room and the same estimate. On a
phone the share menu opens.

![The checklist, the Share button and the «Link copiato» message](08-checklist-condividi.jpg)

**Privacy:** the link contains the room's location and your answers. It goes
through no server: only send it to people you choose.

## 8. Technical details and the map

At the bottom of the column, **Dettagli tecnici** (Technical details) opens with a click.

- **Month and Hour** move the sun on the map: you see where it comes from (amber dashes) and which walls are in sun. The estimate doesn't change: it always covers the whole season.
- **The sun now:** sunrise, sunset, hours of daylight, sun height and direction, and whether the walls are in sun or shaded by a building.
- **Local climate:** the **climate zone** ("legal" if the comune is in the DPR 412/93 table, otherwise "estimated from the climate"), humidity, wind and rain this month, coordinates and square metres.

Example: January at noon, ground floor. The south and west walls are shaded by
the building opposite; the sun saves up to €15 a month.

![January at noon, ground floor: walls in shadow](09-dettagli-piano-terra.jpg)

The same room on the **5th floor or higher**: more sun clears the roofs
opposite and the saving rises to €19 a month.

![The same room on the fifth floor](10-dettagli-quinto-piano.jpg)

## 9. On a phone

On a phone the map fills the screen and the figure always stays visible in the
bottom bar.

<p><img src="11-telefono.jpg" alt="On a phone: the map and the cost in the bottom bar" width="320"></p>

1. Tap **Domande e dettagli** (Questions and details) or the cost at the bottom: the panel opens with search, estimate, questions, checklist, sharing, details and the **Guide** link.
2. The **i** button opens the legend and the drawing hint.
3. The icons bottom right (sun and drop) open the sun and climate readings; tap outside to close them.
4. The panel scrolls vertically only.

## 10. Frequent questions and common problems

| Problem or question | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The range is very wide | Answers are missing: fill in the questions, especially heating, hours and building age. With every answer in, a range remains: that's what can't be seen (walls, habits). |
| The room disappears after a few seconds | It was drawn on water, outside Italy or with crossing sides: read the message and draw it again. |
| "You drew … m²: that's more than a room" | Zoom in, clear with the bin and draw just the room. |
| "Downloading the climate…" stays a long time | The climate or buildings service is slow: wait or try again. |
| The zone says "estimated from the climate" | The comune isn't in the legal table (for example one born from a recent merger): the zone inferred from the climate is used. |
| Location doesn't work | Enable location services for the browser, or type the address. |
| How accurate is the estimate? | Not yet measured as a percentage. Every value has a public source, but the calculation simplifies (20 °C indoors, no heat from people and appliances). Use it to **compare** rooms. |
| Are the prices up to date? | Yes: they update themselves every month from ARERA's files, by part of Italy. The month used is written under the estimate. |
| Is it an energy certificate? | No. For the official energy class ask for the APE. |

For the numbers, formula by formula: [the method](../metodo.md) (in Italian).
